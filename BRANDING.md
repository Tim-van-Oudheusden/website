# Slogan: Reboot With Me
A new-age way of saying: getting your life back on track.
The core principle of the website and project, unfolding in various ways.

# Focus
The focus of this project lies on the following fields:

## Big Tech independence
Follow an up-to-date blog, featuring open source alternatives, self-hosting and Linux as an operating system.
A focus on technologies that work for the average Joe, and not complicated highly technical workflows.

## Meditations, inner peace and selfworth
A focus on mental health for the younger generation (same demographic as me, the Author).
Tricks, recommendations, advice, that sort of stuff.

## Using modern day tools to elevate your capabilities at work.
Highlighting key tools and especially how they can be applied at work. Explicitly not for everyone,
as I can´t work multiple different types of jobs, but somewhat generalized for similair use cases as myself.

## Applying Software Engineering
Stories and tales, success and failures. Everything in my career that might interest or help others.
