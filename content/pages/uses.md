What I actually run, day to day. Most of it has a longer write-up, linked below.

## Hardware

- **Desktop**, running Bazzite.
- **Laptop**: a [Framework 13](https://frame.work/), running Bluefin.

## Operating systems

Both machines run images from [Universal Blue](https://universal-blue.org/): Fedora-based operating systems delivered as container images. More in [My operating system is a container image, yes, really](/articles/my-operating-system-is-a-container-image-yes-really).

- **[Bazzite](https://bazzite.gg/)** on the desktop, for gaming, emulation and streaming to the TV.
- **[Bluefin](https://projectbluefin.io/)** (developer experience) on the laptop, a cloud-native development setup that comes pre-configured.

## Software and development

- **[Flatpak](https://flatpak.org/)** for applications, installed through the [Bazaar](https://github.com/kolunmi/bazaar) app store. More in [Apt-get out of my life, hello flatpak](/articles/apt-get-out-of-my-life-hello-flatpak).
- **[Distrobox](https://distrobox.it/)** for software that is not available as a Flatpak, and for trying out anything more advanced.
- **[Devcontainers](https://containers.dev/)** for development toolchains, instead of layering packages onto the OS.

## Notes and writing

- **[Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/)**, which I have used since its launch in 2020. At work my notes sync through OneDrive with an automated backup to GitHub, and I use [canvas](https://obsidian.md/canvas) for visual explanations. More in [Making my work easier with notes in Obsidian](/articles/making-my-work-easier-with-notes-in-obsidian).
- The articles on this site are written in Obsidian too, and published through the [Obsidian Content Pipeline](/projects/obsidian-content-pipeline).
